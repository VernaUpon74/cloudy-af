Emergency Recovery

Flashes firmware to a device stuck in a boot loop. The flasher waits for the device to enumerate, checks the Product ID, streams the image, restarts and verifies it boots.

Flash stock first. A bricked device must be restored with the original manufacturer firmware before ArcticFox can boot. The bundled stock image is selected automatically; after it succeeds you will be offered the matching ArcticFox build. If no stock image is bundled for your device, choose the manufacturer firmware file manually.
