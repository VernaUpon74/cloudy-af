Emergency Recovery

Flash a firmware image to a device stuck in a boot loop. The flasher waits for the device, checks the Product ID, flashes, restarts, and verifies it boots.

Flash stock first. A bricked device needs its original manufacturer firmware back before ArcticFox can boot. The bundled stock image is picked automatically; after it succeeds you will be offered the matching ArcticFox build. If no stock image is bundled for your device, choose the manufacturer file manually.

Repair Product ID

If a device crash-loops or is not detected after a flash, its dataflash Product ID may be erased and the firmware hangs at boot unable to pick a screen geometry. This rewrites the ID, clears the boot flag, restarts, and verifies the write (NFE "Force PID").
